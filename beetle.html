The Volkswagen Beetle Mini

An absolute classic. I don't even have my license yet but I have been obsessing over these ever since I found out my grandpa had one in the 70s/80s. It's the perfect shape: round, bubbly, and bug-like. Not exactly the safest ride but if I ever get one for funsies, it will definitely be painted baby blue. Or maybe purple, since it is the best color, afterall. Or green, since its the second best color.
